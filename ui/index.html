Trace your floor plan (recommended)

Turn a photo/scan of your blueprint — or just clicks on a plain grid — into the coordinates the engine needs, with no typing of raw numbers required. Nothing here auto-detects walls or rooms — you click where they are, this tool only measures distances and angles from your clicks. Everything it fills in stays visible in the Plot/Rooms fields below, so you can always check or hand-edit it.

  1. Optional: upload a photo of your floor plan (skip this to trace on a plain grid instead).
  2. If you uploaded a photo: set the scale — click two points a known real-world distance apart (e.g. a door), then type that distance.
  3. Trace the flat's outer boundary.
  4. Trace each room, one at a time (repeat as needed).
  5. Set which way is North using the dial.
